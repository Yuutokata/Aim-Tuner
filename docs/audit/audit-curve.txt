== NOTES ==
- Strength search plays click rounds only (flash rounds are not simulated), range 0.70-1.75 is the app range with flash but without the flash rounds.
- A/B uses alpha 0.01 ungrouped (app default), min effect 2.0 pts, max 12 pairs, looked at after every pair; benefit = independent paired difference (12 pairs) of the same curve on the same continuing person.
- Search, A/B and benefit rounds of one seed are played by the same simulated person (adaptation state carries on); the error analysis replays the calibration for every distinct curve so each curve meets the same hand.
- Slope = regression of the binned wanted log gain on ln(speed) (unweighted), median over seeds.
- O2e is applied as a post-filter: a falling best fit needs bicGain >= 2 x 8.0 (equivalent to a second fitAccel run with the doubled threshold for falling fits). O1e drops fits whose unbounded curve falls between 5 and 30 in/s and re-picks the best remaining family (same thresholds).
- Falling/rising of an offered curve: multiplier(30) < multiplier(5) of the offered (bounded) curve; for strengths: best < 1.
- O3 is a re-judging of the same A/B rounds with minEffectPoints 4 for falling curves. O4 benefit is vs plain at the found cm/360, not at base. O5 (mandatory A/B) equals the expor column; the Last search no-A/B rate is the 'last' column.
- In the strength 'right' column RISING needs a rising export, FALLING a falling export; ADAPTED/FLAT rows have no right direction.
- Level check conditions with 'const' play a NoAccel curve at base/factor.
